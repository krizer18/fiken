# Privacy Policy for Fiken

**Last updated: 31 August 2026**

## The short version

Fiken does not collect anything. It has no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, no tracking, and no network connection of any kind.

## What Fiken stores

Fiken saves your settings — paper colour, sound on or off, haptic strength,
go-dark timing, which hand you use, and whether the paper boil is running.
These stay on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never transmitted
anywhere, because Fiken has no way to transmit anything.

Deleting the app deletes them.

## What Fiken does not do

- No personal information is collected, requested, or inferred.
- No usage data, crash reporting, or analytics of any kind.
- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
- No third-party SDKs. Fiken links only Apple's own frameworks.
- No network requests. The app never contacts a server, including ours.

## Device features

Fiken uses the Taptic Engine for haptics, the speaker for optional sound
effects, and the accelerometer to detect a shake (used to reset the cracked
glass screen). Accelerometer readings are used in the moment and never stored
or sent anywhere.

## Children

Fiken is safe for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no
data from children either.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date
at the top.
